# Duration and tank-level results

Detection state, delay and during/after classification matched across all onset repeats in 252/252 settings across both influents and all rates. Each primary duration has 21 runs across 7 distinct tank perturbations. Each heatmap cell represents repeated timing outcomes.

Cells give confirmed runs, median minutes on confirmed runs, and counts during and after the outage. Unavailable means the study had no such channel for the fault. Silent results describe this deterministic grid.
