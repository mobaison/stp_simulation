The engine omits active biomass growth and decay, dissolved oxygen, alkalinity, nitrite, organic nitrogen, and carbon coupling in denitrification. The municipal and default modeled TIN remain above the **10 mg/L** comparative screen. TSS is a mixed-liquor solids proxy. A waste-only solids inventory ratio cannot establish a physical nitrifier sludge age.
